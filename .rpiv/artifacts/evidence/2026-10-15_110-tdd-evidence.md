### Cause

### T1: lock hand-off order

  | variant | order (held, waiting, late) | start deferral |

  | `runPromise(withPermits(Effect.promise))` (65a10bb) | held, late, waiting | op, sync, tick1, tick2 |
  | `withPermits` inside Effect | held, late, waiting | n/a |

  The 65a10bb facade also started a free operation synchronously, where the base deferred it.
