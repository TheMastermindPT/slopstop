### I1 🟡 Note strip canvas has two owners across the legacy-shell layer; the intended one is dead

**Where**
`apps/desktop/src/renderer/theme/seixo.module.css:15` `apps/desktop/src/renderer/theme/seixo.module.css:72` `apps/desktop/src/renderer/projects-workspace.module.css:159` `apps/desktop/src/renderer/workspace-view.tsx:140`

**Code**
```
.seixoTokens {
  background: var(--canvas);
    background: var(--canvas);
        <div className={`${theme["seixoTokens"]} ${styles["noteStrip"]}`}>
```

**Why**
66a29a1 added .seixoTokens as a second selector on the whole .seixo appearance rule, not a tokens-only rule. Its unlayered background (seixo.module.css:72) is the live owner of the note strip background, while the intended owner at projects-workspace.module.css:159 sits inside @layer legacy-shell and never applies on the same element (workspace-view.tsx:140). The two fixes are order-coupled: narrowing .seixoTokens to tokens revives :159, deleting :159 first makes the leak the only owner. The only guard (renderer-foundation.test.ts:452) pins the colour both rules produce, not the owner. Same cascade-order class as the I4 precedent trail.

**Fix**
Split the rule: keep the custom properties in a tokens-only rule shared by .seixo and .seixoTokens, and leave box-sizing, background, color, font and line-height on .seixo alone. The layered background at projects-workspace.module.css:159 then becomes the real owner; the existing seam e2e keeps passing.

Precedent-weighted: this class has now recurred four times (43bc303, ca839da, ef3f92f, 66a29a1).
